Airport Portuguese phrases: what to say when you fly

Na Ponta da Língua · 5 min read

Airports compress a whole trip into a few conversations, and each one has its own words. The good news: the Portuguese you need at an airport is a small, fixed set, and it is the same set every time you fly.

Here it is, in the order you will need it.

At check-in

The counter is the balcão de check-in. If you cannot find it, «Onde é o balcão de check-in da...?» plus the airline name gets you there.

You will be asked «Tem bagagem de porão?», do you have hold luggage. Bagagem de porão goes in the hold; bagagem de mão is the cabin bag you keep with you.

Then the seat question: «Janela ou corredor?» Window or aisle. Answer «janela» or «corredor».

What you receive is the cartão de embarque, the boarding pass.

At security

Security is the controlo de segurança. The two things staff repeat all day are líquidos, liquids, and computador, laptop. Liquids go in a small transparent bag, the laptop comes out of yours.

If a bag gets pulled aside, the phrase you will hear is «Posso ver a sua mala?», may I see your bag. It is routine, not trouble.

At the gate

The gate is the porta de embarque, and screens list it as porta. Boarding is embarque, and embarque prioritário is priority boarding.

The announcement worth recognizing is «última chamada», last call. If you hear it with your flight number, walk faster.

If your trip has a stop on the way, the layover is the escala and the onward flight is the voo de ligação. «Tenho de fazer escala?» asks whether you have to change planes.

On arrival

Arrivals is chegadas, departures is saídas. Baggage claim is the recolha de bagagem, and the belt is the esteira.

The same verb from the pharmacy comes back here: levantar, to pick up. «Onde se levanta a bagagem?» asks where you collect it.

At customs, the green channel is signed «Nada a declarar», nothing to declare. If you have nothing, that is your door.

When something goes wrong

A delayed flight is atrasado; a cancelled one is cancelado. The question to ask is «Qual é o próximo voo?», what is the next flight.

If you miss it, «Perdi o voo» is the sentence, said at the airline desk, the balcão da companhia.

If your bag does not appear, say «A minha mala não chegou» and ask where to report it: «Onde faço a reclamação?» A reclamação here is the report, and making it before you leave the airport matters.

The two patterns that carry you

If you only keep two, keep these:

«Onde fica...?» gets you to anything: a porta de embarque, a casa de banho, a saída.

«É esta a fila para...?» checks you are in the right queue, which in a busy airport is half the battle.

Add «Pode repetir, por favor?» and you can survive every conversation in this article, even the ones you only half understood.
