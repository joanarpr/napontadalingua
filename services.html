More Than Lessons Support for your life in Portugal

Na Ponta da Língua is growing beyond language lessons, offering personalised services for different stages of your journey. Choose what you need — or tell us, and we'll help you find the right starting point.

European Portuguese Lessons Online

One-to-one European Portuguese lessons tailored to your level (from A1 to C2), goals and interests.

We focus on the language you'll actually use, speaking, listening, grammar and everyday situations, whether you're preparing to move, already living here, or simply want to feel more at home in Portuguese. Sessions are online, personalised, and paced around your life.

Portuguese–English Translation

Translation support for personal, professional and everyday communication between Portuguese and other languages.

From emails and documents to messages you want to send with confidence, we help you communicate clearly and appropriately — in Portuguese or English — with the tone and context that the situation calls for.

Seasonal Courses

Short, themed courses and workshops designed around specific topics, seasons and learning goals.

These are focused bursts of learning, festive language, travel Portuguese, or a particular skill, so you can make progress without a long-term commitment. We're currently working on a Christmas vocabulary workshop.

Personal Support in Portugal

Practical, personalised assistance for people adapting to life in Portugal — from navigating everyday situations to understanding local systems and communication.

We can help you prepare for appointments, make sense of letters and forms, practise the conversations you need, and understand how things work locally so daily life feels less overwhelming and more within reach.

English Tutoring

Personalised English lessons for learners who want clear communication, stronger writing and more confidence in everyday or professional settings.

Lessons follow the same student-led approach as our Portuguese classes: conversation, writing or workplace English, adapted to your level and purpose, in a calm space where you can practise and be understood.

Study Support and Techniques

Practical guidance on how to study more effectively — from organising your learning to building habits that help Portuguese stick.
