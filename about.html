About Us More than language lessons

Na Ponta da Língua is a Portuguese language and cultural service created to help people connect with Portugal through language, culture and practical support.

We offer personalised European Portuguese lessons, seasonal courses and a range of services designed for people who are living in, moving to or simply discovering Portugal.

Whether you need to improve your Portuguese, prepare for everyday life, navigate a new environment or communicate across languages, we're here to make the process easier, more personal and more human.

Meet Your Teachers The people behind the lessons

Teacher & Co-Founder Joana Rodrigues

Joana is a native Portuguese speaker and an experienced language teacher with a background in Sociology, urban studies and communication.

She combines a structured approach to grammar and pronunciation with conversation focused on real-life communication. Her lessons are adapted to each student's goals, level and interests, whether they're starting from scratch or looking to refine their Portuguese.

She is particularly interested in the relationship between language, culture and everyday life in Portugal.

Teacher & Co-Founder Nadine Mussá

Nadine, a.k.a. Nikki, is a native Portuguese speaker with a background in Languages, Literature and Culture from NOVA-UNL (NOVA University Lisbon) — and two cats who sometimes like to act as TAs. She has a broad résumé spanning different fields, with a particular focus on writing and, of course, education. She discovered that teaching was one of her passions in 2015, when she started tutoring children and later moved on to teaching adults. She believes that student-led, personalised lessons are one of the best ways to learn a language - along with immersion, of course. One of her biggest achievements was turning a student who hated English and had terrible grades into an English-loving student with stellar ones. Her focus is always on each student’s needs and on building skills such as speaking, listening, writing, reading, and navigating everyday life in Portugal. You’ll find her trying to perfect verb tables or preparing a cultural lesson down to the tiniest detail, because she wants every class to feel like it was made for you. Her lessons are planned, but there’s always room for your input and suggestions, and, most importantly, for you to build confidence and practise your skills in a safe, relaxed environment. She’d love to be a fly on the wall when you say something so natural that your Portuguese friends do a double take. That’s the good stuff. Outside of classes, you’ll usually find her writing or listening to an audiobook... unless she gets distracted by a random YouTube video. She also works as an e-commerce copywriter and is currently translating her book so she can self-publish it. She sees languages not just as subjects, but as a way of seeing the world through new lenses. And, being in Portugal, she knows that while you might get a polite smile from a stranger when you speak English with your friends, you’re much more likely to get a proper response when you turn to them and ask, “Desculpe, tem horas?” even if you still can’t quite hide your accent.

How We Work Learning that fits your life

We don't believe in one-size-fits-all language learning. Every student comes with different experiences, goals and ways of learning.

That's why our lessons combine structure with flexibility. We work on the language you need while also making space for conversation, culture and the situations you're likely to encounter in everyday life.
