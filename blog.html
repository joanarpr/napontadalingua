10 Portuguese Expressions You'll Hear Every Day

Learning Portuguese isn't just about memorising vocabulary and grammar. If you're living in Portugal, you'll quickly discover that some expressions appear everywhere — in conversations with friends, at the supermarket, at work or simply when chatting with your neighbours.

Here are 10 expressions worth knowing:

  1. Olá! — Hello!

    A simple one, but you'll be using it all the time.

  2. Bom dia! — Good morning!

    In Portugal, it's common to greet people when entering a shop, café or other public space.

  3. Está tudo bem? — Is everything alright? / How are you?

    A very common way of asking how someone is doing.

  4. Se faz favor. — Please.

    You'll hear this expression frequently when asking for something.

  5. Com licença. — Excuse me.

    Useful when passing someone, getting someone's attention or entering a space.

  6. Não faz mal. — It's okay / No problem.

    A very useful expression when something goes slightly wrong.

  7. Até já! — See you soon!

    Usually used when you expect to see the person again shortly.

  8. Até logo! — See you later!

    Another everyday way of saying goodbye.

  9. Pois é. — That's right / Indeed / Well, yes...

    A wonderfully Portuguese expression that can have slightly different meanings depending on the context.

  10. Está bem. — Okay / Alright.

    You'll hear this one everywhere — and probably start saying it without even noticing!

These expressions may seem simple, but they're an important part of sounding natural in European Portuguese.
