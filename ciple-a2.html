CIPLE A2 Exam preparation

The CIPLE — Certificado Inicial de Português Língua Estrangeira — is the A2 certificate in Portuguese as a foreign language, issued by CAPLE. We prepare you for it with one-to-one lessons built around the exam, the level you're starting from and the time you have before the test date.

Who it's for

For anyone who needs the certificate for Portuguese nationality, residence, work or study.

You don't need to be at A2 level already to begin. We start from where you are and work towards what the exam asks of you.

What the preparation covers

The four skills the exam tests: listening, reading, writing and speaking.

Alongside them, the grammar and vocabulary you need to communicate at A2 level, and practice with the kinds of tasks the exam puts in front of you.

Everything is adapted to your starting level and to the time available before the exam.

How it works

The preparation is a pack of 10 lessons (€150): two lessons a week, over the five weeks before your exam date.

The calendar is anchored to the exam date, not to general availability — we work backwards from the test so that everything has its place before the day.

Lessons are online, one to one and 50 minutes long, and the pack includes exam simulations.
